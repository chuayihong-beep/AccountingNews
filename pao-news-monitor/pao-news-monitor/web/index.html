      AI usage

      Dates are UTC (Malaysia time minus 8 hours), the same days AI providers bill by.

      Price used for the estimate

      Dollars per 1 million tokens. The default is Claude Haiku 5.5 for prompts up to 100,000 tokens. Most models the tracker uses today are on free plans, so the estimate is what the same work would cost on a paid model, not a bill.

      Mailing list (brief)

      People who get the brief. It's sent from your own Outlook with everyone in BCC (recipients can't see each other). Only admins can see this list.

      Add many at once

      Paste addresses from Outlook, Excel or anywhere - e.g. Jane Tan <jane@icai.in>; bob@cpa.org. Every email address found is added.

      Members (PAOs)

      Changes are picked up by the next daily collection. Deactivated members are hidden and no longer collected; their past items are kept.
